Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains how TechCreative collects, uses, protects, and manages information when you use our website and services.

Last updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to TechCreative. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the information you provide when you interact with our website, contact us, or use our services.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect, why we collect it, and how we may use and protect that information.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with TechCreative, we may collect information such as:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your company or brand name
  • Information you provide through our contact form
  • Details about a project or service you are interested in
  • Messages and other information you voluntarily provide

We only request information that is reasonably necessary for communication and providing our services.

3. How We Use Your Information

Information you provide may be used to:

  • Respond to your questions and enquiries
  • Discuss potential projects
  • Provide requested services
  • Communicate with you about your project
  • Improve our website and services
  • Maintain the security and functionality of our website

We do not use your information for purposes unrelated to your interaction with TechCreative unless we have a legitimate reason or are required to do so by law.

4. Information Sharing

TechCreative does not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

Information may only be shared with trusted service providers when reasonably necessary to operate our website, communicate with you, or provide requested services.

We may also disclose information when required by applicable law, legal proceedings, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of TechCreative or others.

5. Cookies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to improve website functionality, understand how visitors use the website, and improve the overall user experience.

You may be able to control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website functionality.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information provided to us against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no method of transmitting or storing information online can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as it is reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, to provide our services, resolve disputes, maintain records, or comply with legal obligations.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights concerning the personal information we hold about you. These may include requesting access to, correction of, or deletion of certain information.

If you have questions about your information, you can contact TechCreative through our Contact page .

9. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites or services. TechCreative is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of websites that we do not operate.

We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of third-party websites before providing them with personal information.

10. Children's Privacy

Our website and services are not intentionally designed to collect personal information from children without appropriate authorization.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our services, technology, or legal requirements.

When changes are made, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised update date.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact TechCreative through our Contact page.
